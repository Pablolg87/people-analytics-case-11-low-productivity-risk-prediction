# HR4Techies - People Analytics Case 11  
# Low Productivity Risk Prediction

Machine Learning and People Analytics project focused on predicting employees at risk of low productivity using HR, performance, engagement, and organizational data.

---

# Business Objective

The objective of this project is to identify employees with potential low productivity risk in advance, allowing organizations to make more informed and proactive decisions related to:

- Performance management
- Employee engagement
- Workforce planning
- Internal mobility
- Training strategies
- Organizational efficiency

This project combines predictive analytics, explainable AI, and business-oriented interpretation to simulate a realistic People Analytics use case.

---

# Project Scope

This case study includes:

- Synthetic HR dataset generation
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Productivity risk analysis
- Machine Learning model comparison
- Explainable AI using SHAP
- Stacking ensemble model
- Business impact estimation

# Machine Learning Workflow

## 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Initial analysis focused on understanding:

- Productivity distributions
- Organizational patterns
- Correlations between variables
- High-risk employee profiles
- Department-level productivity trends

---

## 2. Predictive Modeling

Several Machine Learning models were trained and compared, including:

- Random Forest
- XGBoost
- LightGBM
- CatBoost
- Stacking Ensemble Models

Evaluation metrics included:

- Accuracy
- Precision
- Recall
- F1 Score
- ROC-AUC

---

## 3. Explainable AI (SHAP)

SHAP values were used to understand:

- Main productivity drivers
- Risk factors influencing predictions
- Feature importance
- Employee-level prediction interpretation

---

# Business Impact

Predicting low productivity risk can help organizations:

- Reduce hidden productivity losses
- Improve workforce efficiency
- Optimize talent management
- Increase employee retention
- Prioritize HR interventions
- Support data-driven HR decision-making
